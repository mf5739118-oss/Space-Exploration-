🔬 Space Science

Space science helps us understand planets, stars, galaxies, black holes and the origin of the universe. Scientists use telescopes, spacecraft and advanced computer simulations to explore the mysteries of space.

🌌 Galaxies

Galaxies contain billions of stars, planets, gas and dust held together by gravity.

🕳️ Black Holes

Black holes are regions of space where gravity is extremely strong and even light cannot escape.

🪐 Planets

Planets orbit stars and can have very different environments, atmospheres and compositions.

⭐ Stars

Stars are enormous balls of hot plasma that produce energy through nuclear fusion.
